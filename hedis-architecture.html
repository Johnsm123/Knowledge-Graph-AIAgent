🗄️ LAYER 3 — NEO4J AURA DB — KNOWLEDGE GRAPH
Graph Nodes
👤 Patient 📋 Measure ⚕️ CareGapRule 🔢 ICDCode 🔢 CPTCode 🔢 NDCCode 🔢 LOINCCode 🧪 LabResult 💉 Procedure 🏥 Diagnosis 💊 Medication ⛔ ExclusionCondition 🕳️ CareGap ✅ Recommendation
Key Relationships
(Patient) -[:HAS_DIAGNOSIS]→ (Diagnosis)
(Patient) -[:HAD_PROCEDURE]→ (Procedure)
(Patient) -[:HAS_CARE_GAP]→ (CareGap)
(Measure) -[:HAS_RULE]→ (CareGapRule)
(CareGapRule) -[:REQUIRES_CODE]→ (CPTCode)
(CareGapRule) -[:EXCLUDED_IF]→ (ExclusionCondition)
(ExclusionCondition) -[:HAS_CODE]→ (ICDCode/CPTCode)
(CareGap) -[:FOR_MEASURE]→ (Measure)
(CareGap) -[:RESOLVED_BY]→ (Recommendation)
(Diagnosis) -[:HAS_CODE]→ (ICDCode)
↓ Rule engine evaluates eligibility & gaps
